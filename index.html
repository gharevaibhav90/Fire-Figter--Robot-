01 / INTRODUCTION

ABOUT OUR PROJECT

The Fire Fighting Robot is a student engineering project that combines mechanical design, electronics and programming. The robot uses two flame sensors to identify flame signals. Arduino processes these signals and controls the movement, servo motor and water pump.

The project demonstrates the fundamentals of embedded systems, automation and robotics in a controlled educational environment.

02 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Understanding the need for an automated fire-response prototype.

🔥

The Challenge

Manual fire response can involve risk and delays. This project explores a small robotic prototype that detects flame signals and demonstrates an automated response using a water pump. It is an educational model, not a certified firefighting device.

06 / SYSTEM OPERATION

WORKING PRINCIPLE

Step-by-step overview of the robot's operation.

01

Flame Detection

Two flame sensors monitor their surroundings and provide signals to Arduino.

02

Signal Processing

Arduino reads the sensor outputs and determines the required response.

03

Movement Control

The L298N motor driver controls the two DC motors as commanded by Arduino.

04

Stopping

When a flame signal is detected, the robot is programmed to stop.

05

Servo Direction

The servo motor moves the water outlet towards the selected direction.

06

Pump Activation

The mini pump is activated through a correctly rated switching driver and suitable power source.

08 / ELECTRONICS

ELECTRONIC CIRCUIT DIAGRAM

Functional connection overview of the Fire Fighting Robot.

Flame Sensors

Left sensor output to A0 and right sensor output to A1. Provide suitable VCC and GND.

Motor Driver

L298N control pins IN1-IN4 connect to D2-D5. ENA and ENB connect to D9 and D10.

Servo Motor

Servo signal connects to D6. Use an appropriate power supply and common ground.

Water Pump

D7 is a control signal only. A suitable external switching driver is required.

Power Supply

Use suitable regulated supplies for Arduino, motors and other components.
